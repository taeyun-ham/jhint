### C-4 컴플라이언스 관리(2칸: 왼쪽 글 · 오른쪽 번호 목록)
- 왼쪽: 작은 머리말 COMPLIANCE GOVERNANCE / 제목 The right evidence for each project. / 버튼 **DISCUSS YOUR REQUIREMENTS →**(→ `/inquiry`)
- 왼쪽 본문: Regulatory requirements vary by formula, manufacturing site, and target market. Before production begins, we verify exact facility credentials for your assigned site and define responsibility for testing, registration, labeling, and import procedures—ensuring every credential matches your final product scope rather than relying on blanket claims.
- 오른쪽:
  - 01 — **Technical & Formula Dossiers:** Full INCI breakdowns, quantitative formulas, Certificates of Analysis (COA), and Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS).
  - 02 — **Safety & Stability Testing:** Product-specific stability, packaging compatibility, microbiological screening, and preservative efficacy (challenge) testing.
  - 03 — **Export & Trade Certificates:** Korean Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) certificates, Certificates of Free Sale (CFS), Certificates of Origin, and embassy legalization support.
  - 04 — **Clear Responsibility Alignment:** Upfront verification of facility credentials and defined roles between JH International, your compliance partners, and your local Responsible Person or importer.
